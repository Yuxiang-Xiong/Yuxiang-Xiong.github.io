Selected Research

Childhood Separation and Family Support Following Parental Functional Limitations

Working paper using the China Health and Retirement Longitudinal Study.

Studies how childhood parental separation is associated with changes in co-residence and other forms of family support when older parents develop functional limitations.

Reconstructed annual parental residence histories and linked life-history records to panel, child and helper rosters; checked inconsistencies in family relationships and distinguished questionnaire skips from unknown responses.

Led the question development, design, data construction, analysis and writing, with research discussions and feedback from Yaohui Zhao.

Technical Standards, Trade and Environmental Outcomes in China

Doctoral dissertation manuscript completed. Examines importing-country technical standards, trade adjustment and environmental outcomes in China.
